By the Scrambler Seat Covers Editors • Updated July 28, 2026 • 6 ranked picks

The term "Scrambler" carries a lot of history. Jeep originally built the CJ-8 Scrambler from 1981 to 1985 — a long-wheelbase open-air pickup version of the CJ that combined trail capability with genuine cargo utility. Today, the Scrambler name has been revived by aftermarket builders and off-road enthusiasts who apply that ethos to modern Wrangler platforms: stretched JK two-doors, JL daily drivers with long-travel suspensions, and flat-bed conversions that echo the original CJ-8 spirit.

Whether you own an original CJ-8 Scrambler, a TJ-era two-door, a JK/JKU with the Scrambler treatment, or a current JL/JLU doing double duty on trail and street, the one thing all Scramblers share is a need for seat covers that can handle open-air abuse. Mud, UV, rain, sweaty trail clothes, and dogs riding shotgun will destroy a stock seat in months. A quality cover changes all of that.

We ranked six options that genuinely earn their place — no filler, no generic universal covers dressed up in marketing language. Our #1 pick is Bartact Tactical Seat Covers, and the remaining five are generation-specific neoprene sets verified to fit real Jeep Scrambler-era and current-generation interiors.

📍 Scrambler Generations at a Glance

Before buying, confirm which platform you're working with. Seat dimensions, mounting points, and airbag requirements differ substantially across generations.

CJ-8 Scrambler
1981–1985
Long-wheelbase open pickup. Flat bench or bucket front seats. Universal or semi-universal covers required. No airbags.
YJ Wrangler
1987–1995
Popular Scrambler donor platform. Narrow front buckets. No airbags. TJ seats are a popular drop-in swap.
TJ Wrangler
1997–2006
Two-door only. Round headlights. Custom-fit neoprene covers available. No side airbags until later years.
JK/JKU
2007–2018
First gen with SRS side airbags. Two-door (JK) and four-door (JKU Unlimited). Airbag-compliant covers required.
JL/JLU
2018–present
Current generation. JL (2-door) and JLU (4-door). Updated seat profile. Best support for custom-fit covers.
Gladiator JT
2019–present
Pickup truck successor to the CJ-8 Scrambler lineage. JL-derived front seats. Distinct rear seat.
Scrambler vs. Gladiator: If you're building a modern Scrambler pickup truck, the Jeep Gladiator JT is the closest factory equivalent to the original CJ-8 concept. Its seat covers use the JL front seat architecture, so JL/JLU front covers apply to the Gladiator JT as well. See our Gladiator seat covers guide for Gladiator-specific rear seat picks.
